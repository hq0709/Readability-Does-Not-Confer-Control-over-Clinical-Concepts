# Scope of the archived figure assets

For the current task—preparing materials for later figure-by-figure or table-by-table remakes while preserving their displayed numbers—there is no outstanding data request. Figure 1's inset values are in `figures/figure-01/` at the paper's precision; numeric Figures 2–8 have their plotted series in their respective final-number folders; Tables 1–28 retain the displayed values. The original figure PDFs and PNGs, including image-bearing Figures 1, 4 and 9, are already committed.

The released [code-repository data bundle](https://github.com/wy-coliney/reading-is-not-writing-code/tree/main/runs) also retains packaged campaign summaries, robustness analyses and selected per-image rows. It is optional for a displayed-value edit; it does not authorize changing the paper's numbers to a different precision.

Only a future request to regenerate an image-bearing figure *from scratch* could call for separate source photos. Figure 9 contains ten qualitative image examples, including CheXpert Plus cases whose underlying dataset is licensed. None of that is required to keep or position its committed PDF/PNG in the manuscript. The [source data guide](https://github.com/hq0709/reading-is-not-writing-code/blob/main/REPRODUCING.md) explains the redistribution boundary.
