\section{Pilot study: registered protocol}
\label{app:seed}

The two-model study that motivated RAW registered the procedures below; Appendix~\ref{app:seed-results} reports its results. The study uses the construction of Section~\ref{sec:framework}, with the differences stated here.

\paragraph{Data, models, and reading.}
We use NIH ChestX-ray14 \citep{wang2017chestxray}, whose findings are mined from radiology reports. A deterministic hash of patient identity assigns rows to disjoint train, validation, and test partitions: 18,212 training images and 5,343 test images from 1,659 test patients. The three cells pair LLaVA-1.5-7B \citep{liu2024improved} with Effusion and Edema, and Qwen2.5-VL-7B \citep{bai2025qwen25vl} with Effusion, at the final visual block consumed by the connector or merger. Hook tests confirm that this block lies on the downstream path, that $\alpha=0$ is a bitwise no-op, and that nonzero strengths change downstream logits. Projection, scaling, and probe follow Section~\ref{sec:directions} with seed 0. Selectivity is Equation~\ref{eq:selectivity} on test rows, with 20 control seeds mapping the 39 recurring input types (AP view, sex, and age decade) to random labels. Selectivity compares the concept with nuisance-type label maps of the same capacity, and the write then tests whether the model uses the concept. Prompts appear in Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}.

\paragraph{Write and strengths.}
The write is Equation~\ref{eq:intervention} along the lifted normal of Equation~\ref{eq:lift}, with every entry of $\boldsymbol s$ floored at $10^{-8}$. The endpoint is the paired mean change in $P(\mathrm{yes})$ from the common unsteered baseline (Section~\ref{sec:ownership}). Original cells use the strengths $\{-1,-0.5,-0.25,-0.1,0,0.1,0.25,0.5,1\}$ on 200 held-out test images. The controls are 20 isotropic random unit directions, one coordinate-permutation sham, and the fixed alternative clinical normals. At the six controlled strengths $\mathcal A=\{\pm1,\pm0.5,\pm0.25\}$, each control receives the same signed steering strength on the same rows. Monotonicity in steering strength over $[-0.5,0.5]$ is reported descriptively.

\paragraph{Original-cell decision rule.}
With $\Delta_c(\alpha)$ the target direction's paired mean change at steering strength $\alpha$, the primary effect and the steering strength that attains it are
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\Delta^\star_c&=\max_{\alpha\in\mathcal A}\operatorname{sign}(\alpha)\,\Delta_c(\alpha),\\
\alpha^\star&=\operatorname*{arg\,max}_{\alpha\in\mathcal A}\operatorname{sign}(\alpha)\,\Delta_c(\alpha).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:seed-rule}
\end{equation}
A cell clears the generic gate when $\Delta^\star_c$ exceeds the largest absolute sham effect over $\mathcal A$ and the 95th percentile of the 20 random directions' changes at $\alpha^\star$, each change multiplied by $\operatorname{sign}(\alpha^\star)$. Because the random directions are evaluated at $\alpha^\star$ alone, the gate is a registered reference with no correction across steering strengths.

\paragraph{Direction-specificity supplement.}
The Nodule response motivated a separately registered supplement at the locked Qwen steering strength $\alpha=+0.25$. The supplement uses 400 ChestX-ray14 test patients absent from the original cohort, with one row per patient chosen by label-blind hash. The supplement estimates the Effusion row of the write matrix (Section~\ref{sec:ownership}). The competitors are the five other pre-existing Qwen normals (Atelectasis, Pneumothorax, Cardiomegaly, Mass, and Nodule), fitted like Effusion, plus 20 random directions and the sham. This gives 28 configurations and 11,200 per-image outcomes. Direction specificity requires an Effusion effect above the random 95th percentile and the absolute sham, and $O_{\mathrm{Effusion}}$ (Equation~\ref{eq:margin}) above zero. Adding competitors can only lower $O_q$. A positive advantage therefore holds for the family tested, and a negative one does not require an exhaustive family.

\paragraph{Causal-ownership matrix.}
A prospective grid crosses six Qwen directions (Effusion, Atelectasis, Pneumothorax, Cardiomegaly, Mass, and Nodule) with their six questions. The grid uses 400 new patients at $\alpha=+0.25$, with 119 random directions and a sham. Question $q$ is owned when $W_{q,q}$ exceeds every other clinical direction, every random direction, and the absolute sham. A separate family of 12 statistics tests shared aliasing. For each direction, these are its signed mean effect on its five off-diagonal questions and its mean excess over the matching diagonals on those questions.

\paragraph{Answer-encoding crossover and Mass confirmation.} A registered crossover uses 200 patients of the causal-ownership cohort, with the other 200 as a patient-disjoint calibration set. The crossover pairs the original yes/no prompt with A-present and B-present prompts, which exchange the finding-to-letter mapping. The writes use $\alpha=\pm0.25$ with six clinical directions, 20 random directions, and a sham. A question enters only if both coded mappings have clean-answer AUROC lower bounds above 0.5 with at least ten patients per class. An independent confirmation then tests Mass on 200 patients absent from every earlier Qwen cohort. The confirmation crosses two wordings (\textit{show}, \textit{is}) with explicit yes/no, A-present, and B-present instructions at $\alpha=+0.25$, with 119 random directions, a sham, and 20 clinical comparisons. Confirmation requires a positive margin against every competitor and Mass effects above every random effect and the absolute sham, across all four coded cells or both original-wording cells.
